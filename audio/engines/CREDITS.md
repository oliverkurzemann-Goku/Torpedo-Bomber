# Recorded piston engine audio — Build 212

Derived loops are credited below. Edits: mono downmix, 28–6500 Hz filtering, slow level correction, selected cuts, Doppler correction for flybys and loop crossfades. Exact cuts, ridge measurements and SHA-256 hashes: [provenance.json](provenance.json). Rebuild with `python scripts/engine-audio/build-recorded-loops.py`. Full-duration public HQ MP3 previews are retained in `source/`; these are not the uncompressed original WAVs.
