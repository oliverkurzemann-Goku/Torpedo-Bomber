OKINAWA BUILD 1

A coast to explore

A separate 16 × 16 km environment study, prepared for Torpedo Squadron. The terrain and coastline use geographic data; vegetation, reef depth and small settlements are artistic reconstructions.

This is a visual prototype, not a surveyed reconstruction of Okinawa in 1945. No missions or saved games are changed.
