Terrain System — DEM/OSM world streaming prototype
Real DEM terrain + real-world OSM content (Step 6) streamed automatically in/out around the camera (Step 4), across the whole generated 25-tile grid — not demo.html's procedural noise, not demo-dem-osm.html's single fixed tile. Fly far enough (past the generated grid's edge, see tools/convert_dem_tile.js's GRID_RANGE) and that direction simply stops streaming in new terrain — that boundary is expected, not a bug (see WorldStreamer.js's heightLoadFailures).
