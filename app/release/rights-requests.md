Subject: Permission enquiry — War Thunder-labelled 3D assets in an independent iPad flight game

Hello,

I am developing an independent WW2 flight game, working title Squadron, for the web and iPad.
Some supplied model files are identified on Sketchfab as War Thunder models, although their
uploaders selected CC BY 4.0. I do not assume that this establishes permission from Gaijin
or the original model/texture creators. Before any further app distribution, could you confirm

The attached asset schedule identifies the runtime filenames, source model IDs and SHA-256
hashes. It includes P-47D-28, Bf 109 F-1, Fw 190 F-8 custom payload, Ju 87 B-2, Me 262 A-1a/Jabo,
Me 163 B, SBD-3, F4U-1C, M16 MGMC, 8.8 cm Flak 37 Sfl., Jagdpanther and M4A1 (76) W.

The requested scope is incorporation of meshes and all associated textures in a compiled or
packaged independent game, web delivery, iOS/iPadOS installation, TestFlight/external testing,
App Store distribution and promotional screenshots/video, worldwide. Please specify separately
whether personal prototypes, free public distribution, paid apps, advertising and in-app
purchases are permitted; no monetization has been decided.

Please also clarify permission for resizing textures, separating parts, adding animated landing
gear/flaps and other adaptation, the required attribution, duration, fees, any restrictions, and
any additional third-party permissions. The app can be reverse engineered; I cannot promise
that embedded model files are impossible to extract. I am not requesting rights to resell a
standalone asset pack or to imply official endorsement.

If permission is unavailable, I will arrange independently licensed replacements for app release.
Please let me know who can provide an authoritative written response.

Thank you,
Oliver Kurzemann
