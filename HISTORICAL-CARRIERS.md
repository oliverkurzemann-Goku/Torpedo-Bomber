# Historical carriers: USS Enterprise (CV-6) and IJN Kaga (Build 240)

Both carriers replace USS Midway and Zuihō in the Pacific game (`torpedo-carrier.html`). The text below is the package
README delivered with the models; the section "In the game" at the end records how they are wired in.

Both models represent June 1942. All geometry and paint are generated by the accompanying project scripts. The GLBs use metres, +Y upward, +Z toward the bow and -X toward starboard. Both islands occupy the starboard side.

| Measured feature | Enterprise CV-6 | Kaga |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main hull length | 246.74 m | 247.65 m |
| Waterline length | 234.70 m | 240.30 m |
| Waterline beam | 25.374 m | 32.50 m |
| Flight deck | 244.4496 × 26.2128 m | 248.58 × 30.48 m |
| Draught | 7.911 m | 9.48 m |

Enterprise's published maximum length includes projecting fittings and differs from her hull and flight-deck lengths. The model retains separate dimensions for each. Kaga's published deck width varies slightly by source. This model uses the 30.48 m figure in the Japanese technical summary.

The principal dimensions are numerically constrained. Hull stations, flight-deck heights above the loaded waterline, platform outlines, equipment shapes and the positions of minor fittings are reconstructed from period photographs and published profiles. The result is a game-scale historical reconstruction. These details have no independently established dimensional tolerance.

Enterprise has a tall starboard island, a single funnel, two masts, three centerline elevators, two deck catapult tracks, arresting wires, open side hangar bays, eight single 5-inch mounts, four quadruple 1.1-inch mounts and 32 single 20 mm mounts. Kaga has her small starboard island, a downturned funnel, three differently sized elevators, wood and steel deck areas, bow recognition disc, stern stripes, open aft galleries, eight twin 127 mm mounts, eleven twin 25 mm mounts and ten casemate gun shapes. Mount locations are reconstructed from photographs and profiles.

The root metadata retains the published dimensions, historical date, sources, coordinate frame, exhaust outlets and elevator locations. The exported geometry is checked by `okinawa/tests/historical-carriers.cjs`, including the actual island vertices, hull, waterline and flight deck. The Khronos validator must report zero errors and zero warnings.

Build with Node.js and the project's dependencies installed.

```sh
node scripts/build-historical-carriers.cjs
node okinawa/tests/historical-carriers.cjs
```

The included Blender scenes are imports of the same GLBs used for the rendered views. Their neutral studio lights are provided for inspection. The JavaScript generators are the editable construction source.

## In the game (`torpedo-carrier.html`)

| | Enterprise | Kaga |
|---|---|---|
| file | `assets/ships/enterprise-cv6-1942.glb` | `assets/ships/kaga-1942.glb` |
| role | the player's carrier on all attack sorties (`CARRIER_MODEL_URL`) | the player's carrier in Zero defence sorties (`IJN_CARRIER_URL`) and the capital-ship target (`cruiser`) |
| scale / yaw | 1 / bow to +X (`CARRIER_YAW`) | 1 / bow to +X; as target scaled to ~250 m (`CRU_TARGET_LEN`) |
| flight deck in the model | y = 16.85 (`CARRIER_DECK_Y_MODEL`), equal to the game deck height, so the waterline is the sea surface | y = 21.2 (`IJN_DECK_Y_MODEL`); the game deck stays at 16.85, so as a deck she rides 4.35 m deeper than designed (as a target she floats at her real draught) |
| landing strip | 244.4 x 26.2 m (the old Midway deck was 35 m wide) | 248.6 x 30.5 m |
| exhaust outlets | 6 (`shipExhaust` nodes) | 1, downturned |

The models carry the contracts the game already reads: `shipVisualOnly` on decorative nodes (not part of the collision/deck
surface), `shipExhaust` outlets for funnel smoke, and `asset.extras.markers` (`flight-deck`, `aircraft-elevator`,
`island-starboard`, `gun-mount`). Deck markings and wires sit up to 5 cm (wires 10 cm) above the flat deck plane and are visual only.
`DeckActivity` takes the real deck half width (`deckHalfWidth`) and keeps the crew on the deck edge strips.

The generators are `scripts/build-historical-carriers.cjs` (+ `scripts/lib/carrier-paint.cjs`) on top of
`scripts/build-own-ships.cjs`; `node okinawa/tests/historical-carriers.cjs` checks the exported dimensions, the starboard island,
the flat deck, the triangle/draw-call/texture budgets and the glTF validator. A rebuild is not byte-identical to the delivered
GLBs (the encoder differs by a few bytes between machines); the delivered files are the reference
(`sha256`: Enterprise `9079bc14...8772`, Kaga `5f2b2ded...58c2a`). The Blender scenes and preview scripts of the package are
not part of the repository (they are imports of the same GLBs).
